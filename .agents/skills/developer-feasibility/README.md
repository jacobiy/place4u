# Developer Feasibility

Fast feasibility for a ground-up deal. Give it a land price and your cost, rent, and financing assumptions, and get back the cost to deliver, the stabilized value, the development spread, whether the take-out loan covers itself, and the land price the deal actually supports at your target margin, with a build-or-pass verdict at the end.

Most land deals die on the spread and the residual land value. This skill runs that math in one pass, in whatever assistant you already use, so you can screen a site before you build a full model. No code required.

## Use it

1. Copy the body of [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) into your assistant (a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Custom GPT, or a fresh chat).
2. Give it the land price and your assumptions: cost per unit, rents, financing terms, exit and market cap rates, and your target profit margin on cost. The skill invents none of these; they are yours.
3. Paste the deal and read the four sections plus the verdict.

## What is intentionally left to you

The assumptions that decide the deal (your cost basis, achievable rents, cap rates, and target margin) are inputs, not defaults. This open version does the arithmetic and leaves the judgment calls to you. If you want feasibility running automatically off your standing assumptions every time a land deal lands, that is what NextAutomation builds.
