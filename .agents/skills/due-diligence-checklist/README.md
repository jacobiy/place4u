# Due Diligence Checklist

Run a structured, time-boxed acquisition due-diligence workflow. Give it your LOI and your data-room index, and get back a populated tracker: every checklist item with an owner and a status, a running clock on the DD period, and the red flags rated and pulled to the top so you act on them while there is still time.

The window between LOI and close is short, and a deal breaks when a critical flag surfaces on day 28 instead of day 8. This skill loads your checklist, matches it against what the seller has actually delivered, and keeps the whole thing on a clock, in whatever assistant you already use. No code required.

## Use it

1. Copy the body of [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) into your assistant (a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Custom GPT, or a fresh chat).
2. Give it your checklist (or ask it for a generic starting one to edit), then paste your LOI and your data-room index. It reads the statuses from what the data room actually holds, never from hope.
3. Read the header clock, the tracker, and the critical-flag list.

## What is intentionally left to you

The checklist itself (your workstreams and items) is yours to supply and own; the skill runs your list, not a canned one, and marks anything without a document as Pending rather than guessing. It organizes and surfaces; a person confirms and resolves every item, and it never contacts the seller or a third party. If you want diligence tracked automatically off your standard checklist on every deal under LOI, that is what NextAutomation builds.
