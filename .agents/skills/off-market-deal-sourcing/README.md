# Off-Market Deal Sourcing

Build an off-market prospect list from public records. Give it a submarket, your deal box, and your own list of public signals, then paste the parcel and ownership records you have, and get back a ranked shortlist of owners worth a first conversation, each with the public fact that put it on the list and the person named behind the entity.

Off-market flow is a records problem before it is an outreach problem. This skill turns your deal box and your signals into a repeatable pass over public data, so the shortlist is built the same way every time and you spend your outreach time on the owners that actually fit.

## Use it

1. Copy the body of [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) into your assistant (a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Custom GPT, or a fresh chat).
2. Give it your deal box and your own signal list: the public-record conditions you treat as a reason to look closer, and the bar for landing on the shortlist. The skill ships with no signals and no weights on purpose; those are yours to set.
3. Paste the parcel and ownership records you have and read the ranked table.

## What is intentionally left to you

The signal set, the weighting, and the shortlist bar are not baked in. Which public conditions actually predict a sale, and how much each counts, is where a sourcing edge lives, so this open version leaves all of it as placeholders and asks you to supply it. It also works only from records you paste in: it never scrapes, skip-traces, or enriches, and it never contacts an owner. If you want a version wired to live data sources on your standards, that is what NextAutomation builds.
