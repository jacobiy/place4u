# LP Reporting

Draft a quarterly LP report from the actuals you supply. Give it the quarter's numbers and the operator's notes, and get back the standard investor-update sections written in a plain house voice, with a hard grounding gate: every number in the prose has to trace to an input you gave it, or the draft comes back marked for review.

Quarterly reporting eats a day per deal, and the real risk is a fluent report with a number that is not quite right. This skill turns your actuals into a first draft, in whatever assistant you already use, and refuses to print a figure it cannot trace to your inputs. No code required.

## Use it

1. Copy the body of [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) into your assistant (a Claude Project, a ChatGPT Custom GPT, or a fresh chat).
2. Give it the quarter's actuals, the operator's notes, and your deal name and period. The skill invents no number; anything it needs but was not given comes back as a labeled placeholder.
3. Read the status line first, then the draft, then the grounding flags. Drop the sections into your own branded template, review every line, and send it yourself.

## What is intentionally left to you

Your branded template, your voice specifics, and the send itself stay with you. The skill drafts sections in a plain, honest register and gates them on grounding, but a person reviews every line and sends the report; it never contacts an LP. If you want quarterly reports drafted automatically off your actuals, in your template, the moment the numbers close, that is what NextAutomation builds.
